Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What ASChat is

ASChat is a private, self-hosted application operated by its owner for personal and small-team use. It is not offered to the general public.

Google user data

  • Access requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only. ASChat can see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself; it cannot read any other file in the Google Drive.
  • Use: uploading media files (photos, videos, voice notes, documents, stickers) from the owner's conversations into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive, creating share links for those files, and downloading them back when needed.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own server. The Drive file IDs and links are stored in the owner's own database.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

ASChat's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/aspirelabaz/aschat-site.
